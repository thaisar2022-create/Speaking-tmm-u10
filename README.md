# ThaiSar — Unit 10 Version 4 · အလုပ်အကိုင်များ (Occupations)

**Textbook source:** `Speaking book (28-9-24).pdf`, printed pages **62–69**. Unit 11 starts on p.70.

Mobile-first Thai listening and speaking PWA for Myanmar learners. Matches the school’s deep-purple/yellow colors, logo, and the latest Unit 3 V4 interaction design.

## Lesson content
- **120 vocabulary cards** covering the three illustrated occupations/person vocabulary pages (printed 62–63, 65) and a limited glossary from Chapter 10 sentences
- **73 distinct sentence cards** including all 31 textbook standalone examples and the 19 textbook Q&A sets (4 pairs include additional printed answer lines)
- **19 textbook Q&A sets**, each spoken question and answer shown individually in Learn, plus book-printed alternative answer sentences where provided
- **24 expandable grammar patterns** derived from constructions appearing in Unit 10 examples; these headings are instructional and are not claimed to be printed grammar headings
- **12 Unit 10 quiz questions**, **10 four-option Listen & Fill exercises**, **10 rearrangement tasks** with Thai/book-style Latin phonetics/Myanmar sound cues
- Native mobile speechSynthesis Thai playback (normal and slow), record and replay, browser-local lesson progress, installable-PWA assets.

## Important for school review
- The printed book has some OCR-incompatible glyphs, and the picture vocabulary is manually transcribed; please review **Thai spellings, precise roman phonetics, Myanmar pronunciation, and meanings** against the original PDF before use with students.
- Printed example 3 uses `วิศวากร` (nonstandard orthography) rather than `วิศวกร`. We retained the PDF's spelling in the sentence entry and use the standard form in vocabulary/Q&A; please confirm the school’s preferred spelling.
- Printed Q&A 7, 8, 9, and 11 have extra answer sentences; all are included as alternate answers, not newly composed.
- The source phonetic notation is represented with tone marks and slash-delimiters following the school system. Single-word readings on picture pages are manually transcribed or adapted when necessary, so **teacher review is essential**.
- The browser's Thai TTS is device-dependent, not native-speaker recordings or a precise tone grader.
- Thai and Arabic digits are spoken as complete Thai number words (e.g., ๑๕ → สิบห้า).
- Progress is stored on the device only. Serve over HTTPS for PWA installation; opening local `index.html` is a preview, not full installation.
